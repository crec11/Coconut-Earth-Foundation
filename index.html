01 / The opportunity

From a meaningful mission to a fundable plan.

A prospective funding partner should be able to connect the environmental need, proposed intervention, delivery model, and expected outcomes. The Foundation's underlying project details and evidence should be inserted here before publication.

01 — NEED

The challenge

Define the specific environmental problem, affected geography, and scale of need. Support any market or impact figures with dated sources.

02 — RESPONSE

The intervention

Describe the Foundation's actual activities, target beneficiaries, delivery partners, and how the approach differs from alternatives.

The case for support

02 / Operating & funding model

Show exactly how funding becomes action.

The financial mechanism must be described accurately. A charitable donation, grant, recoverable grant, loan, and equity investment have materially different rights and outcomes.

03 / Evidence & traction

Use the following structure for a dated and verifiable record of execution. No completed milestones are claimed in this prototype.

Evidence to add

Evidence to add

04 / Strategy & capital deployment

A clear path from pilot to repeatable impact.

Illustrative planning stages below should be replaced by the Foundation's approved dates, budget, dependencies, and milestones.

Stage 1 — Validate

Document the intervention, establish baseline measures, confirm operating partners, and define project economics.

Stage 2 — Demonstrate

Deliver measurable project results, report expenditure against budget, and evaluate what worked.

Stage 3 — Scale responsibly

Expand only where delivery capacity, funding resilience, governance, and measured outcomes support growth.

Use of funds

Publish approved allocations across program delivery, staffing, infrastructure, measurement, and working capital. Do not present illustrative percentages as fact.

Funding milestones

Connect each funding tranche to specific deliverables, evidence requirements, and accountability checkpoints.

05 / Impact framework

Measure the change, not just the activity.

Environmental

Define environmental indicators and the methodology used to measure them.

Community

Document who benefits, how benefits are assessed, and any unintended effects.

Economic & operational

Show costs per outcome, operating resilience, and relevant local economic benefits.

06 / Governance & due diligence

Trust is built through transparency.

Leadership & oversight

Publish verified leadership biographies, relevant experience, board responsibilities, and governance practices.

Investor / funder resources

Provide an approved organizational overview, financial statements, project reports, impact methodology, and material risk disclosures.

Common due diligence questions

Is this an equity investment opportunity?

The funding instrument has not been verified from the supplied website. Confirm the entity's legal status and any actual offering before describing an opportunity as equity investment.

What financial information is available?

Historical financials, budgets, forecasts, and funding targets should be supplied and approved by the Foundation. None are invented on this page.

How are outcomes verified?

Include project-level measures, baseline methodology, reporting frequency, and any third-party assurance where available.

What risks should funders consider?

Relevant risks may include delivery, fundraising, governance, regulation, measurement, and long-term sustainability. Tailor disclosures to the actual projects.
